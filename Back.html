Back Training

Build width and thickness with these powerhouse compound movements.

Conventional Deadlifts

3 Sets 5-8 Reps
  1. Stand with feet hip-width apart, mid-foot under the barbell.
  2. Bend at the hips and knees to grip the bar with a flat back.
  3. Drive through your heels and stand up straight, keeping the bar close to your shins.
  4. Lower the bar back to the floor with control, maintaining a neutral spine.

Bent-Over Barbell Rows

3 Sets 8-12 Reps
  1. Hold a barbell with an overhand grip, knees slightly bent.
  2. Hinge at the waist until your torso is nearly parallel to the floor.
  3. Pull the bar toward your lower ribs, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
  4. Slowly lower the weight back to the starting position.

Wide-Grip Lat Pull-downs

3 Sets 10-12 Reps
  1. Sit at the machine and grip the bar wider than shoulder-width.
  2. Lean back slightly and pull the bar down toward your upper chest.
  3. Focus on pulling with your elbows and engaging your lats.
  4. Slowly return the bar to the top for a full stretch.

One-Arm Dumbbell Rows

3 Sets 12 Reps Per Arm
  1. Place one knee and hand on a bench for support, keeping your back flat.
  2. Hold a dumbbell in the opposite hand, hanging straight down.
  3. Pull the dumbbell toward your hip, keeping your elbow close to your body.
  4. Lower the weight with control and repeat for the other side.
