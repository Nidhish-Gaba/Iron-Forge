Bicep Training

Build peak height and thickness with these essential arm movements.

Routine Progress

0%

Standard Barbell Curls

3 Sets 8-12 Reps
  1. Stand tall with your feet shoulder-width apart, holding a barbell with an underhand grip.
  2. Keep your elbows tucked close to your torso at all times.
  3. Exhale and curl the weight toward your shoulders while contracting your biceps.
  4. Inhale and slowly lower the bar back to the starting position.

Dumbbell Hammer Curls

3 Sets 10-12 Reps
  1. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with a neutral grip (palms facing your body).
  2. Keep your upper arms stationary and curl the weight forward.
  3. Focus on the "peak" contraction at the top of the movement.
  4. Lower the dumbbells back down with control to the starting position.

Concentration Curls

3 Sets 12 Reps Per Arm
  1. Sit on a bench with your legs spread and lean forward slightly.
  2. Rest your elbow against the inside of your thigh for stability.
  3. Curl the dumbbell up toward your chest, focusing solely on the bicep contraction.
  4. Slowly lower the weight back down and repeat for the other arm.

Weighted Chin-Ups

3 Sets To Failure
  1. Grab the pull-up bar with an underhand grip, hands shoulder-width apart.
  2. Pull your body upward until your chin is over the bar.
  3. Concentrate on using your biceps rather than just your back to pull.
  4. Lower yourself slowly until your arms are fully extended.
