The Iron Forge Rules

  • Red Meat: Limit red meat to 1-2 times a week to keep digestion efficient.
  • Omega-3s: Ensure you have fatty fish (Salmon/Mackerel) at least twice a week for joint health.
  • Meal Timing: Consume your largest carbohydrate meal within 2 hours of finishing your workout.
