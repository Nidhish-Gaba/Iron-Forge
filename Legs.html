Leg Training

Build explosive power and massive strength in your lower body.

Barbell Back Squats

4 Sets 8-10 Reps
  1. Place the barbell across your upper back, gripping it firmly with both hands.
  2. Feet should be shoulder-width apart, toes slightly pointed out.
  3. Inhale as you hinge at the hips and bend your knees until thighs are parallel to the floor.
  4. Exhale and drive through your heels to return to a standing position.

Walking Lunges

3 Sets 12 Reps Per Leg
  1. Stand tall with feet hip-width apart, holding dumbbells at your sides if desired.
  2. Take a large step forward with your right leg, lowering your hips until both knees are at 90 degrees.
  3. Ensure your front knee does not pass your toes and keep your torso upright.
  4. Push off your back foot and step forward to repeat on the other side.

Machine Leg Press

3 Sets 12-15 Reps
  1. Sit in the machine and place your feet shoulder-width apart on the platform.
  2. Lower the platform slowly toward your chest until your knees are at a 90-degree angle.
  3. Exhale and push the platform away using your quads and glutes.
  4. Avoid locking your knees out completely at the top of the movement.

Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts

3 Sets 10-12 Reps
  1. Stand with feet hip-width apart, holding dumbbells in front of your thighs.
  2. Hinge at your hips, pushing them back while keeping a very slight bend in your knees.
  3. Lower the weights along your shins until you feel a deep stretch in your hamstrings.
  4. Drive your hips forward to return to the starting position, squeezing your glutes.
