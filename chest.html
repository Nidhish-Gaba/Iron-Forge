Chest Exercises

A complete routine to forge a powerful and defined chest.

Routine Progress

0%

Barbell Bench Press

3 Sets 8-10 Reps
  1. Lie flat on your back on a standard weight bench.
  2. Grip the barbell with your hands slightly wider than shoulder-width.
  3. Inhale and slowly lower the bar until it lightly touches your mid-chest.
  4. Exhale as you push the bar powerfully back to the starting position.

Incline Dumbbell Press

3 Sets 10-12 Reps
  1. Set a weight bench to a 30-45 degree incline.
  2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand and sit back on the bench.
  3. Press the weights up over your chest, keeping your wrists stable.
  4. Lower the weights slowly until level with your chest, then press back up.

Dumbbell Flys

3 Sets 12-15 Reps
  1. Lie flat on a bench holding dumbbells above your chest, palms facing each other.
  2. Keep a slight bend in your elbows to protect the joints.
  3. Inhale and lower your arms in a wide arc until you feel a deep stretch.
  4. Exhale and squeeze your chest to bring the weights back to the center.

Push Ups

3 Sets To Failure
  1. Start in a high plank position with hands slightly wider than shoulders.
  2. Engage your core and keep your body in a straight line.
  3. Inhale as you lower your body until your chest is just above the floor.
  4. Exhale and push back up to the starting position.
