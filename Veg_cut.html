Shredding Principles

  • Carb Tapering: Consume most of your carbs in the morning and around your workout; keep dinner carb-free.
  • Sodium Control: Reduce salt intake to prevent water retention and look "dryer" and more vascular.
  • Black Coffee: Use it as a natural fat burner and appetite suppressant (limit to 2 cups).
