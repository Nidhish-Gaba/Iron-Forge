Tricep Training

Target the "horseshoe" muscle for massive arm thickness and pushing power.

0%

Tricep Dips

3 Sets 10-15 Reps
  1. Grip the parallel bars and push yourself up to the starting position with arms locked.
  2. Inhale and slowly lower your body by bending your elbows.
  3. Lower yourself until your elbows are at a 90-degree angle.
  4. Exhale and push back up powerfully to the starting position.

Overhead Dumbbell Extensions

3 Sets 12 Reps
  1. Sit or stand tall, holding one dumbbell with both hands directly above your head.
  2. Lower the weight behind your head by bending only at the elbows.
  3. Keep your upper arms stationary and close to your ears.
  4. Exhale and use your triceps to extend your arms back to the starting position.

Cable Tricep Push-downs

3 Sets 12-15 Reps
  1. Attach a bar to the high pulley and grip it with palms facing down.
  2. Tuck your elbows into your ribs and keep them fixed there.
  3. Push the bar down until your arms are fully extended.
  4. Slowly return the bar to chest level, maintaining tension.

Diamond Push-ups

3 Sets To Failure
  1. Get into a push-up position but place your hands together under your chest.
  2. Form a diamond shape with your index fingers and thumbs.
  3. Lower your chest toward your hands while keeping elbows close to your body.
  4. Push back up to the start, squeezing your triceps at the top.
