Abs Training

Build a stable, powerful midsection with these essential movements.

0%

Abdominal Crunches

3 Sets 15-20 Reps
  1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat on the floor.
  2. Place your hands lightly behind your head or across your chest.
  3. Curl your upper body toward your knees, contracting your abs.
  4. Lower back down slowly, keeping tension on the core throughout.

Forearm Plank

3 Sets 45-60 Secs
  1. Place forearms on the floor with elbows aligned below shoulders.
  2. Extend legs behind you, resting on your toes.
  3. Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
  4. Engage your core and hold for 30–60 seconds without dropping your hips.

Hanging Leg Raises

3 Sets 10-12 Reps
  1. Hang from a pull-up bar with an overhand grip, arms straight.
  2. Keep your legs straight and engage your lower abdominals.
  3. Slowly lift your legs until they are parallel to the floor.
  4. Lower them back down with control, avoiding any swinging motion.

Russian Twists

3 Sets 20 Reps (Total)
  1. Sit on the floor with your knees bent and feet flat or slightly elevated for a challenge.
  2. Lean back slightly while keeping your spine straight to engage your core.
  3. Clasp your hands together and rotate your torso from side to side.
  4. Touch the floor on each side beside your hips, maintaining a controlled pace.
