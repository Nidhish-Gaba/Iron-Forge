Cardio Training

Complete every movement to forge elite endurance.

Heart’s racing, goals chasing. Cardio conquered! 🏆

Running

  1. Maintain an upright posture.
  2. Land softly on the mid-foot.

Cycling

  1. Adjust seat height correctly.
  2. Pedal in smooth, circular motions.

Jumping Jacks

  1. Swing arms fully above head.
  2. Stay on the balls of your feet.

Jump Rope

  1. Rotate from wrists only.
  2. Jump just 1-2 inches off floor.

Burpees

  1. Drop into a deep squat.
  2. Explode upward into a jump.

Mountain Climbers

  1. Keep wrists under shoulders.
  2. Drive knees rapidly to chest.
