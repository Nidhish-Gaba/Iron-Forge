Shoulder Training

Build boulder shoulders and overhead strength with these key lifts.

0%

Barbell Overhead Press

3 Sets 8-10 Reps
  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, holding a barbell at shoulder level.
  2. Brace your core and press the bar directly overhead until arms are locked.
  3. Keep your head tucked back as the bar passes, then push it forward "through the window."
  4. Slowly lower the bar back to your upper chest under control.

Dumbbell Lateral Raises

3 Sets 12-15 Reps
  1. Stand tall holding dumbbells by your sides, palms facing inward.
  2. With a slight bend in your elbows, lift the weights out to your sides.
  3. Pause at shoulder height to feel the contraction in your side delts.
  4. Lower the weights back down slowly to the starting position.

Dumbbell Front Raises

3 Sets 12 Reps
  1. Hold dumbbells in front of your thighs with palms facing your body.
  2. Lift one weight directly in front of you until it is slightly above shoulder level.
  3. Keep your arm straight and avoid swinging your body for momentum.
  4. Lower slowly and repeat with the other arm.

Face Pulls

3 Sets 15 Reps
  1. Set a cable machine to face height with a rope attachment.
  2. Pull the rope towards your forehead, pulling the ends apart as you go.
  3. Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the peak of the movement.
  4. Return to the starting position with a controlled stretch.
